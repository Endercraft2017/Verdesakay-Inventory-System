PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before we talk.

Is OTOM ready for my company?

That depends on the task. OTOM is in active development. A walkthrough checks current capabilities and gaps; a pilot proposal defines the exact scope you can evaluate.

Can you bring in our existing records?

Bring a safe sample or a description of the format. We will check the available import path, cleanup and migration work before including it in a proposal.

Who handles errors, setup and support?

We agree who enters, reviews and approves information, how corrections are handled, and what support is included in the written pilot or subscription terms.

Does the mobile view need an installed app?

The shown interface is a mobile web app. Device, connectivity and permission-dependent tasks still need to be tested for your intended setup.

What about payroll compliance, AI and integrations?

This page does not establish statutory payroll correctness or live external-provider behavior. These requirements need explicit verification, qualified review where needed, and agreed scope.

How do pricing and payments work?

We quote the relevant subscription, setup and support after scope. Current source supports manual bank or GCash payment reporting and owner confirmation. Payment instructions and timing belong in your written agreement.

What if we need a custom system instead?

AFK³ also scopes custom software, automation and defined operational services. Choose “Custom software or automation” in the inquiry form. A guided evaluation is useful when you need a specific supported workflow checked; compare current established supplier offers if immediate self-service or managed payroll is essential.
