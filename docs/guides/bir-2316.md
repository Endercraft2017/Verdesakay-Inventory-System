# BIR Form 2316 (year-end certificate)

The **Certificate of Compensation Payment / Tax Withheld** every employee receives at
year-end, and that the employer files with the BIR.

## What it's for

- Give each employee their 2316 by **January 31**.
- File the employer copies with the BIR by **February 28**.
- It shows the employee's total compensation for the year and the tax withheld.

## Before you start

- **Accounting → Tax & Compliance → BIR 2316** (needs Accounting: view).
- The figures come from the **payslips generated for that year** — so run all your
  cutoffs and Generate Payslips for the whole year first.

## Steps

1. Open **Accounting → Tax & Compliance → BIR 2316** and pick the **year**.
2. The table lists every employee with payslips that year: gross compensation, taxable
   amount, tax due, tax withheld, and the **over / (under)** difference (positive = the
   employee is owed a refund; negative = tax still to collect).
3. **Print** on a row opens that employee's certificate. Review, then **Print…** to paper
   or PDF.
4. **Export CSV** downloads the whole list for your records or your accountant.

## It's a worksheet — finish it before filing

Payroll tracks basic pay, overtime, holiday pay and the statutory deductions. It does
**not** track:

- **De-minimis benefits** (rice, uniform, medical allowance, etc.)
- **Allowances** (representation, transportation, COLA, housing)
- **A previous employer's** compensation and tax withheld for staff hired mid-year

Add those to the printed certificate (or supply them via the API options) and
**cross-check every figure against the official BIR 2316 form** before you issue or file.
The tax due is computed with the current annual tax table; a big year-end swing usually
means an allowance or de-minimis amount is missing.

## Customising the layout

The certificate is rendered from the **"BIR 2316"** template under **Documents →
Templates** — edit it there (company header, wording, extra lines) like any other
document template.
