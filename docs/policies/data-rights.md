# Access, correction and deletion requests

DRAFT — privacy contact, applicable rights and response deadlines must be approved.

Contact your workspace administrator or [OWNER: privacy channel]. Give your workspace, request type (access/export, correction, deletion, restriction or objection) and a safe reply address. Do not send passwords, government identifiers or full payroll files. If you cannot use your account, the privacy owner will arrange proportionate identity verification through an existing trusted channel.

We record receipt and jurisdiction, verify identity and authority, identify affected systems, and explain the applicable response deadline [OWNER]. Access/export must exclude password hashes, session/reset credentials, other users' data and confidential anonymous-report authorship. Corrections to HR/payroll or financial records may require approval and an auditable adjustment rather than overwriting history.

Deletion is reviewed against employment, tax, accounting, dispute and legal-hold duties [OWNER: approved retention schedule]. We explain any refusal or retained subset and available appeal/regulator route [OWNER]. Approved erasure includes databases, uploads, search indexes, configured processors and device copies where controllable. Backup expiry and restoration suppression must be tracked so deleted data is not silently reintroduced. This is an operator-managed procedure; no automatic whole-account deletion is currently promised.
