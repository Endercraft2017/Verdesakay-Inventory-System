Set up your company’s email

Your company uses its own mailbox or SMTP provider. Email is disabled until an administrator configures it. Our platform’s Brevo notification account is separate from your company’s outgoing email.

Before you start

  • You need the Manage users (users.manage) permission to configure email. Ask your company administrator if Settings is unavailable.
  • Your plan must include the Email module. If Email is missing, contact your system provider.
  • Have your provider’s SMTP host, port, username, app password or SMTP key, and authorized From address ready. A domain name alone does not provide email service.

Configure and test

  1. Open Email in the right-hand tools rail, then Settings.
  2. Set Send email via to SMTP.
  3. Enter your From name and From email. The provider must permit sending from this address. Reply-to is optional.
  4. Enter the SMTP host, port, username, and SMTP password or key from your provider. For port 587, leave SSL unchecked (STARTTLS). For port 465, check SSL (implicit TLS).
  5. Enter an address you control under Send a test email to, then click Save & test. This saves your settings and sends a real message.
  6. Confirm the message arrives; check spam as well. An accepted test means the provider accepted it, not guaranteed inbox delivery. Review Email → Sent log if needed.

Passwords are encrypted when stored and are never displayed again. Leave the password field blank on later edits to keep the existing password. Set transport to Off to disable sending. Sending requires the separate mail.send permission.

Sending from your own domain

Verify your sender or domain with your provider. Ask the person managing your domain’s DNS to add the provider’s SPF and DKIM records and configure DMARC. Do not create multiple SPF records; merge authorized senders as your provider instructs. Wait for verification before testing. Sending through an unrelated mailbox without domain authorization can lead to spam or rejection.

Hosting requirements

The server hosting your workspace must allow outbound SMTP on your provider’s port. If it blocks SMTP, contact your system provider about hosting options. Our platform Brevo HTTPS connection does not enable tenant SMTP automatically.

Troubleshooting

  • Email not configured: select SMTP and fill in the host and From address.
  • Login rejected: check the SMTP username and key, app-password eligibility, and whether SMTP AUTH is allowed. Your usual account password may not work.
  • Connection failed: confirm the host, port, SSL setting, and your provider’s network restrictions.
  • Sender rejected: verify the From address/domain with your provider.
  • Accepted but not received: check spam, provider delivery logs, domain authentication, and provider sending limits.
